## Sources

The files pane, at the bottom left, lists the viewer's sources. A source is one entry in
this list: a single image, or a whole sequence. `j` / `k` move the cursor; `Enter` (or a
click) shows the source under it.

`J` / `K` move a source down or up the list. The list's order is also the order of the
tiled view and of `n` / `p`. `d` drops a source from the viewer.

`e` turns a sequence into one source per frame (up to 500), for example to tile frames
side by side, and back.

## Annotations

Above the files sits a **global** row: [annotations](annotations.md) there go over every
image. Each row shows how many annotation lines it has; `l` goes into a row's lines and
`h` comes back out. `D` `D` clears every annotation (inside a row: just its own).

Drag the pane's title up to show more rows.
